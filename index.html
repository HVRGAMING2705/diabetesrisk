💊 DiabetesRisk — Diabetes Risk Predictor

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A logistic regression model trained on 768 synthetic health records modelled on the classic Pima Indians Diabetes dataset predicts the risk of type-2 diabetes from 8 health facts: pregnancies, glucose, blood pressure, skin thickness, insulin, BMI, family history (diabetes pedigree function) and age. 5-fold CV: accuracy = 76.3%, AUC = 0.81 vs a majority-class baseline of 67.2%.

How it works: each value is standardised (subtract the training mean, divide by the standard deviation), multiplied by the model's learned weight, and summed with a bias; a sigmoid turns the total into a probability of diabetes. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try it: 6 pregnancies, glucose 180, BMI 38.5, age 55, pedigree 0.85 → about 98.4% risk. A 23-year-old with glucose 85, BMI 22.0, pedigree 0.17 → about 1.1% risk. A borderline case — glucose 140, BMI 31, age 42 → about 55.8% risk.

Honest note: this model was trained on synthetic data modelled on the classic Pima dataset patterns, so it is a classroom demo — it is not a medical diagnosis. Always consult a doctor for real health decisions.
